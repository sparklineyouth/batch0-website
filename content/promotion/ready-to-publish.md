## Claims to avoid

- No guaranteed grants, investment, admissions outcomes, revenue, or finished companies.
- No “four-week program” claim; use the current program calendar.
- No “last chance tonight” or countdown unless the current application deadline actually says that.
- No invented alumni, mentors, attendance, testimonials, press, or scarcity.
- No blanket “free program”: the starter kit and application are free; accepted students pay tuition.
- Avoid prices in reusable posts so regional pricing and active promotions stay accurate on the website.
